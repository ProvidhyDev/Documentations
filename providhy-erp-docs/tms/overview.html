TMS Service

The TMS-Service is the tenant-management system: tenant creation and lifecycle (create, company setup, verify/status, deactivate, delete), subscription and package/contract management (upgrade, renew, grace period, credit subscription), subscription payments (with /cms/ variants for the CMS platform), release notes, and cross-service tenant lookups.

Repo: TMS-Service @ branch f1-production, SHA 56f95765. Routes on f1-production: 109 total, 108 latest-version. Groups: /api/tenant/ (80), /api/payment/ (21), /api/release-notes/ (5), /api/dashboard/ (3). Source: config/urls.py, tenant_management/views.py (single 204KB module), payment/views.py, apps/release_note/views.py, dashboard/views.py @ 56f95765.
