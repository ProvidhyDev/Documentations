TMS . Subscription Payment

Payment flow (verified at class level)

  1. The tenant (or CMS) initiates an upgrade/renew/resubscribe payment request.
  2. The endpoint talks to the payment provider (eSewa) and records the transaction; the provider callback updates the transaction status.
  3. Internal status endpoints (txn-status-internal :358, credit-payment-internal :664) let other services (e.g. the ERP modules checking subscription expiry, the payment service) query or credit transactions.
  4. On success the subscription/contract records under /api/tenant/ are updated (see tenant upgrade & renew flows).

Per-endpoint request/response field tables are unverified (the 98KB views module was not fully read line-by-line) - the auth map above is verified.

Frontend usage

tenantPortal-frontend drives the tenant-side payment endpoints; the /cms/ and internal routes are consumed by the CMS platform and other services respectively.
