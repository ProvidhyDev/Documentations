Configuration . Dashboard: Fiscal Year and Master Defaults

The dashboard app (dashboard/views.py @ df75e09b) owns the fiscal-year engine and the platform master-data defaults.

Fiscal year engine

# Method Path View Purpose
1 GET/POST /dashboard/fiscal-year/ (+detail, delete) FiscalYearAPIView (+Detail/Delete) FY master CRUD
2 POST /dashboard/update-tenant-fiscal-year/ CreateFiscalYearForAllTenantsAPIView roll FY out to all tenants
3 POST /dashboard/update-iam/ UpdateFiscalYearDataIam sync FY into IAM user data
4 POST /dashboard/add-fiscal-year-info/ AddFiscalInfoToTenantEntries add FY info to tenant entries
5 POST /dashboard/close-fiscal-year/ ManualFiscalYearClosureView manual closure (locks tenant)
6 POST /dashboard/fiscal-year-change/ ChangeTenantFiscalYearApiView switch tenant to new FY
7 POST /dashboard/transfer-fiscal-year/ CreateTenantFiscalYear create tenant FY + carry forward
8 GET /dashboard/tenant-fy-list/ GetTenantFiscalYearLIst tenant FY list
9 GET /dashboard/test-fiscal-year-lock/ TestFiscalYearLock verify lock state (ops)
10 POST /dashboard/tenant-fy/clean-active/ CleanTenantActiveFyAPIView clean duplicate active FY rows (ops)
11 GET /dashboard/get-fiscal-year-from-date/ GetFiscalYearFromDateAPIView date to FY resolution (other services)
12 POST /dashboard/create-previous-fiscal-year/ CreatePreviousFiscalYearAPIView back-fill previous FY
13 POST /dashboard/transfer-payment-terms/ TransferPaymentTermsAPIView carry payment terms into new FY
14 GET /dashboard/tenant-fy-essence/ TenantFYEssenceExposeView internal FY essence for services
15 GET /dashboard/tenant-fy-info/ TenantFYEssenceExposeFrontEndView tenant FY info for apps-frontend

Auth: cms-iam on the master-FY CRUD and roll-out/closure operations; IAM/internal on tenant-facing reads (per-view lines unverified at def for the internal group - confirm before relying).
