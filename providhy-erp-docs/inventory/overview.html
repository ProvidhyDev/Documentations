Business purpose

The product master and stock engine. It owns products (with UOM, unit conversions, batches, categories, campaigns), the stock ledger (Inventory + BatchInventoryTotal), and the only manual stock correction (stock adjustment, two generations in the code). Sales deliveries and purchase receipts decrement/increment stock through this service's stock API; stock reports and the inventory valuation in reporting read the ledger. Adjustments go through the tenant's approval flow and post a stock-adjustment journal into Accounting. (Product variant / product-variant and deduction functions are excluded per scope; see the exclusion table below.)

ProductManagement . Overview

Django / DRF microservice that owns the product master (products, categories, UOMs, campaigns, batches), the stock ledger (inventory and stock adjustments) and a small set of shared dashboard endpoints. Four apps are mounted from core/urls.py: product at /api/product/ (core/urls.py:8), inventory at /api/inventory/ (core/urls.py:9), dashboard at /api/dashboard/ (core/urls.py:10) and the Django admin under settings.ADMIN_URL (core/urls.py:19). The service is both user-facing (ERP screens) and service-facing: the Sales and Purchase services drive the entire stock ledger through internal, password-gated endpoints.

Catalog: 127 routes total - 110 latest, 17 superseded (catalog/ProductManagement.json). Versioning rule applied: where both a v1 and a v2 / Revamped endpoint exist, the v2 / revamped handler is the latest and the v1 route is listed as superseded.

Authentication model

Two IAM auth backends in dashboard/auth_backends.py:

  • IAMAuthenticationBackendProduct (auth_backends.py:23-51) - for /api/product/: the IAM token must grant ServiceChoices.PRODUCT_MASTER and the user must be email-verified; the token is validated via POST {IAM}api/token/verify/ (validate_token_with_iam, auth_backends.py:85-95); request.auth becomes the tenant id.
  • IAMAuthenticationBackendInventory (auth_backends.py:54-82) - for /api/inventory/ (and the adjustment / dashboard-inventory routes): requires ServiceChoices.INVENTORY, same token validation.
  • APIKeyAuthentication (auth_backends.py:195-243) - X-API-Key header Fernet-decrypted must equal the tenant id; used by the CDXE product list (products.md route 23).
  • InternalRequestPermission (auth_backends.py:139-147) - S2S gate: the shared internal password must be present in the body or query; authentication is skipped.

HasPermission (auth_backends.py:98-130) is the feature/action gate for IAM-authenticated endpoints:

  1. Any non-GET request from a tenant with subscription_expired is rejected with 403.
  2. Tenant root users (ISRoot, auth_backends.py:133-136) are allowed.
  3. Otherwise the service calls GET {IAM}api/internal-check-permissions/ with the internal password and user id (auth_backends.py:106-108, helper.IAM_PERMISSION_CHECK helper.py:69) and requires both the view's required_feature_permission and required_action_permission.

EbillingBlockPermission (auth_backends.py:246-253) blocks ebilling-subscribed tenants from the revamped stock-adjustment endpoints.

Feature permissions (dashboard/choices.py): PR901 PRODUCT_CREATION (choices.py:199), PR902 PRODUCT_CATEGORY (:200), PR903 UOM (:201), PR904 VARIANT (:202 - excluded feature), PR905 PRODUCT_CAMPAIGN (:203), PR1001 STOCK_ADJUSTMENT (:205), PR2301 CAMPAIGN_CREATION (:251). Actions (choices.py:114-120): add_edit, view, approve_cancel, active_inactive, export, open_close.

Not used in ProvidhyERP - not documented

  • /api/product/variant/search/

  • /api/product/variant-wise/
  • /api/dashboard/set-variant-path/
  • /api/inventory/grn-manipulation/
  • /api/inventory/grn-return-manipulation/

Plus the GRN deduction / stock-in helper functions behind the two GRN routes - GRNInventoryCheckManipulation (inventory/views.py:3583-3701), GRNReturnInventoryCheckManipulation (inventory/views.py:4169-4275) and the grn_inventory_ins_increment / grn_inventory_ins_decrement helpers (inventory/custom_func.py:44-59) - not documented.

Side effects on the excluded feature that occur inside documented endpoints (noted in the relevant pages): product create/update syncs a ProductVariant row when variants are passed (product/serializers.py:1093-1102), the migration import writes Variant rows (product/views.py:3953-3969), and tenant data deletion removes Variant rows (dashboard/views.py:248-250).

Frontend usage

Per fe_usage.json, 23 of the 110 latest routes are referenced by the apps-frontend production scan (a floor, not a ceiling): the product master list/search/detail endpoints, the UOM endpoints, the category list endpoints, the product recent-transactions timeline, the duplicate-batch pre-flight check, the product-batch detail, plus /api/dashboard/system-choices/ and /api/dashboard/analytics/. Everything else is internal S2S (Sales / Purchase / Accounting / CDXE / Kafka consumers) or not covered by the scan.
