ProjectMaster . Miniapp Party Endpoints

Federated (eSewa miniapp) customer surfaces: the miniapp creates/reads customer parties, searches them, and validates customer connections - all under /api/miniapp/. Source: miniapp/views.py, miniapp/urls.py @ 07c3ea60.

# Method Path View (views.py) Auth
1 POST /miniapp/customer/ AddCustomer (:18) FederatedIdentityAuthentication
2 GET/PUT /miniapp/customer/ RetrieveEditCustomer (:71) auth unverified at def (federated expected)
3 GET /miniapp/customer/search/ CustomerSearchView (MiniAppFilterMixin, :210) FederatedIdentityAuthentication
4 GET /miniapp/dummy-customer-check/ DummyCustomerCheck (:255) auth unverified at def (debug route)
5 POST /miniapp/validate-customer-connection/ ValidateCustomerConnection (:265) auth unverified at def
6 POST /miniapp/validate-miniapp-customer/ ValidateMiniAppCustomer (:318) auth unverified at def
  • The miniapp (federated identity via IAM) manages a lightweight customer record for its users; validation endpoints let the miniapp confirm a customer exists and is connected before deeper flows.
  • The dummy route (:4) is a debug artifact - do not use in production integrations.
