# Method Path View Auth
1 POST /lead/cms/ LeadCreateView cms-iam
2 GET/PUT /lead/cms/ LeadRetrieveUpdateView cms-iam
3 GET /lead/cms/search/ LeadSearchView cms-iam
4 POST /lead/bulk-validation/ BulkLeadValidationView cms-iam
5 POST /lead/bulk-add/ BulkLeadCreateView cms-iam
6 GET /lead/get-lead-email/ InternalLeadSearchView internal (auth unverified at def - email pipeline)
7 POST /lead/fonepay-lead/ CaptureFonePayLeadAPIView public (fonepay widget lead capture; auth unverified at def)
8 GET/POST /lead-group/cms/ (+pk), /lead-group/cms/search/select/ LeadGroupListCreateView, LeadGroupSearchSelectView cms-iam
9 GET /lead-group/internal-retreive/ InternalLeadGroupRetrieveView internal (auth unverified at def)
  • Leads are the marketing pipeline: public widgets (contact form, fonepay) capture leads; CMS manages them, groups them, and bulk-imports from spreadsheets (validate-then-create two-phase).
  • The internal retrieve endpoints serve the email/bulk pipeline in the events service.
