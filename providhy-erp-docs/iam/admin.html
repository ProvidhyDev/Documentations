Notes

  • Permission model entities: FeaturePermission / NewFeaturePermission / NewActionPermission (heimer_auth/models.py:61-110), groups CustomUserGroup (models.py:111), users CustomUser (models.py:134). Permission checks for other services are evaluated from the user_permissions claim carried in the JWT.
