IAM . Federated External Identity

The federated_identity app (federated_identity/) implements the external-party identity surface: eSewa merchants, miniapp users, and other external providers register and authenticate against IAM, and Providhy services query their entities/activities internally.

Models: ExternalEntity (federated_identity/models.py:18) and EntityActivity (models.py:52). Auth backends: FederatedIdentityAuthentication (federated_identity/auth_backends.py:10) and MiniAppAuthentication (auth_backends.py:37) - the miniapp auth used by the eSewa miniapp flows.

  • Registration (views.py:103-109): external providers register/refresh their entity mapping (user link, provider code, entity type) - serializer ExternalEntityRegistrationSerializer (serializers.py:32). Throttling class LoginRegisterThrottle (federated_identity/throttling.py:4) applies to login/register routes.
  • Token (views.py:348-354): mints an external-token pair for the federated identity; verify/refresh serve the other services and the external platform.
