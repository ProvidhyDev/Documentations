Business purpose

The general ledger and the single money truth of the platform. It owns each tenant's chart of accounts (seeded from platform defaults at onboarding), the four voucher types (journal, payment, receipt, contra - one model, one lifecycle), bank accounts, and bill settlement. Module services (Sales, Purchase, stock adjustments) never write ledger rows directly: they call Accounting's internal journal hooks, and the approved vouchers are what the books show. The fiscal-year close (get-opening-info / transfer-closing) locks the old year and carries closing balances forward, with reporting keeping opening-history for audit.

Accounting Module

The Accounting service owns the general ledger for every tenant: chart of accounts (COA) and groups, the voucher family (journal, payment, receipt, contra), bank accounts, bill settlement, dashboards, and fiscal-year operations. It also exposes transaction-linked journal hooks that Sales/Purchase call to create and cancel journals against their transactions.

Repo: Accounting @ branch f1-production, SHA a60aaba3. Routes scanned: 102 total, 102 latest-version routes documented in this module. Apps: chart_of_accounts, journal_voucher, payment_voucher, receipt_voucher, contra, bank, bill_pay_receive, dashboard, account_payable, account_receivable.

Authentication model

  • IAM - IAMAuthenticationBackend + HasPermission with per-feature permissions (choices.AllFeatures.JOURNAL_VOUCHER, PAYMENT_VOUCHER, RECEIPT_VOUCHER, CONTRA_VOUCHER, BANK_ACCOUNT, CHART_OF_ACCOUNT, BILL_PAYMENT_RECEIVE, ...) and per-method actions (VIEW / ADD_EDIT / DELETE). Pattern verified at e.g. journal_voucher/views.py:38-40, payment_voucher/views.py:44-46, receipt_voucher/views.py:33-35, contra/views.py:37-39, bank/views.py:24-26, bill_pay_receive/views.py:32-34, chart_of_accounts/views.py:57-59.
  • internal - the transaction-linked hooks (dashboard/create-journal, dashboard/cancel-journal, account-payable/, account-receivable/) and service lookups (get-account-detail, coa-list-reporting, list-bank-account-multiple) are internal-authenticated for other services (per-view classes; see pages).
  • Voucher lifecycle mirrors the Sales/Purchase pattern: create -> submit for approval (tenant-configured basic/custom flow via Configuration service) -> approve/reject/cancel, with notifications, activity log, and Kafka record events.
