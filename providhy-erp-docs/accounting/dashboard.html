Fiscal year operations

  • get-opening-info (COA page :25) + transfer-closing (:26) implement fiscal-year close: fetch opening balances, then transfer closing balances into the new year.
  • add-fiscal-year (dashboard :9) registers the tenant's new fiscal year with Accounting.
